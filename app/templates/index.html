Three stages. One stronger story.

Each WorkProof challenge follows the same structure so employers can assess execution, reasoning and response to change.

01 / BUILD40%

Make it work.

Build a practical solution to a role-specific workplace scenario. Attach code or a repository when you can.

Can you deliver?
02 / EXPLAIN30%

Explain your choices.

Describe your approach, trade-offs, and why the solution fits the brief — in your own words.

Do you understand it?
03 / ADAPT30%

Handle what changes.

Respond to a changed requirement and show how you would adapt the solution responsibly.

Can you adapt?
